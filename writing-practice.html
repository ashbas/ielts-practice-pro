ESSAY PRACTICE

15 IELTS-style Task 2 questions

These are original practice questions created for IELTS preparation. They are not official IELTS questions.

Education

  1. Some people believe schools should focus more on practical skills than academic subjects. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  2. Some people think students should be allowed to choose the subjects they study from an early age. Others believe a broad curriculum is better. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  3. Online education is becoming increasingly common. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Technology

  1. Artificial intelligence is likely to change many types of work. Is this a positive or negative development?
  2. People spend more time communicating through technology and less time communicating face to face. Is this a positive or negative development?
  3. Some people believe children should not use smartphones until they are teenagers. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Environment

  1. Some people believe individuals can do little to improve the environment and that governments and large companies must take responsibility. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  2. Many cities are becoming more crowded. What problems does this cause, and what solutions can you suggest?
  3. Some people think economic growth is more important than protecting the environment. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Work & society

  1. Some people believe working from home benefits employees and employers. Others believe people work better in an office. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  2. Job satisfaction is more important than earning a high salary. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  3. In many countries, people are working longer hours than in the past. Why is this happening, and is it a positive or negative development?

Health & lifestyle

  1. Governments should spend more money encouraging healthy lifestyles than treating illness. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  2. People are becoming less physically active because of modern technology. What are the causes and what solutions can you suggest?
  3. Some people think individuals are responsible for their own health, while others believe governments should provide more support. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

A SIMPLE PLAN

Plan before you write

1

Position

Decide exactly what you think. Your position should answer the question directly.

2

Two main ideas

Choose two strong ideas that support your position. Avoid trying to cover everything.

3

Explain & example

Develop each idea with an explanation and a relevant example.

4

Check

Leave a few minutes to check grammar, spelling, articles, plurals and verb forms.

WRITING STRATEGY

Clarity beats complexity.

Don't force complicated vocabulary into every sentence. Clear ideas, accurate grammar, logical organization and relevant examples matter more.
