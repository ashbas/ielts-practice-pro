TASK 1 PRACTICE

Describe a change in a chart or process.

Start by identifying the largest overall trends, then select the most important comparisons and supporting details.

Plan your Task 1 response →Review the Task 1 structure →

Task 1 planning

Spend a few minutes identifying the overall trend, two or three major features, and the comparisons that best support your overview.

Task 1 structure

Use an introduction, a clear overview, and two body paragraphs containing grouped comparisons and relevant data.

TASK 2 PRACTICE

Some people think success is mainly achieved through hard work.

To what extent do you agree or disagree? Plan your position, supporting reasons and examples before you begin writing.

Plan your essay →Review a suggested essay structure →View useful vocabulary →

Task 2 planning

Write your position in one sentence, choose two strong reasons, and think of one specific example for each reason.

Task 2 structure

Use an introduction, two developed body paragraphs, and a conclusion that clearly restates your position without copying the introduction.

VOCABULARY

Useful academic phrases

For example: One important factor is... · This is because... · For instance... · On the other hand... · Overall...

COMMON MISTAKES

Check before you finish.

Look for missing articles, subject-verb agreement, repeated vocabulary, unclear pronouns, weak paragraph structure and sentences that do not directly answer the question.

WRITING TIP

Answer the question you were asked.

A well-organized essay with relevant ideas is more valuable than memorized phrases that don't naturally fit the question.
