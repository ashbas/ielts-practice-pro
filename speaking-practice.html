SPEAKING PRACTICE

Practice answering
without memorizing.

Use these questions to practice speaking naturally. Give yourself a few seconds to think, then answer aloud rather than writing your response.

Part 1Part 2 Cue Cards

PART 1

Familiar topics

Try to answer each question in 2–4 sentences. Give a reason or small example instead of stopping after a short yes/no answer.

Home & neighbourhood

  1. What do you like most about the place where you live?
  2. Is your neighbourhood a good place for families?
  3. Would you like to live somewhere different in the future?
  4. What is one thing you would improve about your neighbourhood?

Work & studies

  1. What do you enjoy most about your work or studies?
  2. What part of your work or studies do you find difficult?
  3. Do you prefer working alone or with other people?
  4. What would you like to learn in the future?

Free time

  1. What do you usually do when you have some free time?
  2. Did you have the same hobbies when you were younger?
  3. Do you prefer spending free time indoors or outdoors?
  4. Is there a hobby you would like to try?

Technology

  1. What piece of technology do you use most often?
  2. Has technology changed the way you communicate?
  3. Do you think you spend too much time on your phone?
  4. What technology would you find difficult to live without?

PART 2

Cue card practice

Prepare for one minute. Speak for up to two minutes. Use the bullet points as prompts, but don't feel you must answer them in exactly the same order.

01

Describe a person who has influenced you.

You should say:

  • who this person is
  • how you know them
  • what influence they had on you
  • and explain why their influence was important.
02

Describe a place you would like to visit.

You should say:

  • where it is
  • what you know about it
  • what you would do there
  • and explain why you would like to visit.
03

Describe something useful you learned.

You should say:

  • what you learned
  • when you learned it
  • how you learned it
  • and explain how it has helped you.
04

Describe a memorable journey.

You should say:

  • where you went
  • who you went with
  • what happened during the journey
  • and explain why you remember it.
05

Describe a time you helped someone.

You should say:

  • who you helped
  • what the situation was
  • what you did
  • and explain how you felt afterwards.
06

Describe a book, film or programme you enjoyed.

You should say:

  • what it was
  • when you experienced it
  • what it was about
  • and explain why you enjoyed it.

PART 3

Develop your ideas

For Part 3, aim for a clear opinion, a reason, and an example or explanation.

Education

  1. What skills should schools teach that are not always included in the curriculum?
  2. Is practical experience more valuable than academic knowledge?
  3. How might education change over the next twenty years?

Technology & society

  1. How has technology changed people's social lives?
  2. Do the benefits of artificial intelligence outweigh its disadvantages?
  3. Should children have limits on their use of digital devices?

Work & success

  1. What makes someone successful in their career?
  2. Is job satisfaction more important than salary?
  3. How might workplaces change in the future?

SPEAKING STRATEGY

Extend your answer naturally.

A useful pattern is answer → reason → example. For example: state your view, explain why you feel that way, then give a brief personal or general example.
