Original IELTS-style questions for Parts 1, 2 and 3. Speak your answer aloud and use the timer for realistic practice.

Part 1 Questions

Aim for two or three natural sentences.

Where do you live?
What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
What kind of music do you enjoy?
Do you prefer mornings or evenings?
How often do you use public transportation?

PART 2 CUE CARD

Describe a place you would like to visit.

Include where it is, why you want to visit, what you would do there and how you would feel.

Part 3 Questions

Develop your answer with an opinion, reason and example.

Why do people enjoy travelling?
How can tourism affect local communities?
Do you think travel will become more sustainable?
