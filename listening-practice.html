SECTION 1 · FORM COMPLETION

Community Centre Course Registration

Listen to the conversation between a caller and a community centre receptionist. Complete questions 1–5.

This V1 exercise uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis, so no audio files or server are required.

Receptionist: Good morning, Riverside Community Centre. How can I help you?

Caller: I'd like to register for a photography course. I saw it advertised online.

Receptionist: Certainly. We have an evening course starting on the 14th of October. It runs for six weeks, every Tuesday evening.

Caller: That sounds good. What time does it start?

Receptionist: Classes begin at 6:30 and finish at 8:00. The fee is 85 dollars, which includes the use of basic equipment.

Caller: Great. And where is the class held?

Receptionist: In Room 4 on the second floor. You'll need to bring your own memory card.

Caller: No problem. I'd like to book a place, please.

Receptionist: Of course. Can I take your name?

Caller: It's Priya Shah. That's P-R-I-Y-A, Shah, S-H-A-H.

LISTENING STRATEGIES

Before you press play

1

Prediction

Look at each blank and predict whether you need a number, name, place, day or other type of information.

2

Distractors

Listen for corrections and changes. A speaker may mention one detail and then replace it with the correct information.

Aa

Spelling & Numbers

Pay close attention to names, dates, prices, room numbers and other details that must be written accurately.

3

Keep moving

If you miss an answer, don't stop listening. Move to the next question and keep your attention on the recording.
