PART 1 PRACTICE

Answer naturally and directly.

Try these questions aloud. Aim for two or three sentences rather than one-word answers.

Practice questions →Part 1 strategy →

Part 1 questions

Where do you live? →What do you enjoy doing in your free time? →Do you prefer spending time alone or with other people? →

Part 1 strategy

Give a clear answer, add one reason or example, and avoid memorized speeches.

CUE CARD PRACTICE

Describe a place you would like to visit.

Use one minute to prepare. Make brief notes about where it is, why you want to go, what you would do there, and how you would feel.

Start 1-minute preparation →Start 2-minute speaking practice →View a sample Band 8-style answer →

1-minute preparation

Write four short notes: where, why, what you would do, and how you would feel. Do not write a full script.

2-minute speaking practice

Speak continuously for up to two minutes. If you finish early, expand with an example, comparison or personal detail.

Sample answer approach

A strong answer develops ideas naturally: introduce the place, explain your reasons, describe what you would do there, and finish with a personal reflection.

PART 3 PRACTICE

Develop your ideas.

Give an opinion, explain why you think that way, and support it with an example or comparison.

Practice discussion questions →Part 3 strategy →

Part 3 questions

Why do people enjoy travelling? →How can tourism affect local communities? →Do you think travel will become more sustainable in the future? →

Part 3 strategy

Use a simple structure: opinion → reason → example → conclusion. You do not need to give a complicated answer.

SPEAKING TIP

Don't memorize every answer.

Prepare ideas, vocabulary and structures you can adapt. A natural answer is usually more useful than a perfect script.
