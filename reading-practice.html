PASSAGE

Urban Green Spaces

As cities grow, parks and other green spaces can provide residents with places for recreation. Researchers have also examined their possible effects on temperature, air quality and wellbeing. However, creating and maintaining such spaces requires land, funding and long-term planning.

TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

Questions

1. Urban green spaces can provide places for recreation.
2. All researchers agree that parks improve air quality.
3. Green spaces require resources to maintain.
4. Every city has increased its number of parks.

Answers

1. True — the passage states that parks provide places for recreation.
2. Not Given — the passage says researchers have examined possible effects, but does not say all agree.
3. True — maintenance requires land, funding and planning.
4. Not Given — no such comparison is provided.
