IELTS VOCABULARY

Use better words.
Not just harder words.

Learn useful academic vocabulary in context. The goal is accurate, natural word choice—not memorizing long lists.

Significant

Meaning: important or large enough to have an effect.

Example: There has been a significant increase in the number of people working remotely.

Consequently

Meaning: as a result of something.

Example: Public transport became less reliable; consequently, more people chose to drive.

Decline

Meaning: a gradual decrease or reduction.

Example: The report shows a decline in participation among younger adults.

Encourage

Meaning: to give someone support or a reason to do something.

Example: Schools can encourage students to develop healthy habits.

Reliable

Meaning: consistently good or dependable.

Example: Governments need reliable data when making long-term decisions.

Address

Meaning: to deal with a problem or issue.

Example: Cities must address the shortage of affordable housing.

Substantial

Meaning: large in amount or importance.

Example: The project requires a substantial investment of public money.

Widespread

Meaning: existing or happening across a large area or among many people.

Example: Smartphone use has become widespread among adults.

QUICK QUIZ

Choose the best word

1. The government introduced new measures to _____ the problem of traffic congestion.

2. There has been a _____ increase in the cost of housing.

VOCABULARY TIP

Learn collocations, not isolated words.

Instead of memorizing “significant” alone, learn natural combinations such as “significant increase,” “significant impact” and “significant change.”
