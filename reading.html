TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

Look for evidence, not keywords.

True means the passage agrees with the statement. False means it contradicts it. Not Given means the passage does not provide enough information to decide.

MATCHING HEADINGS

Find the paragraph's main idea.

Read the paragraph for its overall purpose before matching a heading. Avoid choosing a heading simply because it repeats a word from the paragraph.

MULTIPLE CHOICE

Eliminate before you choose.

Find the relevant part of the passage, compare it with every option, and eliminate choices that are only partially correct or unsupported.

MATCHING INFORMATION

Scan systematically.

Identify the distinctive detail in the question, scan for paraphrases, and confirm the answer by reading the surrounding sentences.

READING STRATEGY

Don't read every word at the same speed.

Use skimming to understand structure, scanning to locate details, and close reading when a question requires precise evidence.

20-minute practice

Spend 3 minutes skimming a passage, 14 minutes answering questions, and 3 minutes checking your evidence and spelling.

Skimming technique

Read the title, introduction, first sentence of each paragraph, and final sentence. Build a quick mental map before searching for answers.

Scanning technique

Predict distinctive words or concepts in the question, then scan the passage for paraphrases rather than expecting exact wording.

READING TIP

Answers need evidence.

When you think you've found an answer, go back to the passage and confirm the meaning rather than relying only on a keyword match.
